Found a problem, or have a question or an idea about the game? Write to: alystor@tuta.io

What to write about

  • Game problems — sound, display, or anything that does not work as expected

Numerel is fully offline and has no account or sign-in, so email is the only way to reach the developer. Please mention the app version (shown on the “for parents” screen inside the app) and the device you are using.
